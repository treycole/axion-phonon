Here
   CSG = crystallographic space group
   CPG = crystallographic point group
   MSG = magnetic space group
   MPG = magnetic point group
   UMPG = unitary subgroup of magnetic point group

-----------------------------------------------------
Pyrochlore
-----------------------------------------------------
In the high-symmetry reference structure,
  CSG Fd\bar{3}m  (227)       CPG m\bar{3}m
  MSG Fd\bar{3}m' (227.131)   MPG m\bar{3}m'   UMPG m\bar{3}

  The point symmetry of the Ir (Wyckoff 16c) atom is
     CPG \bar{3}m   (no free params)
     MPG \bar{3}m'

After the distortion, the reduced symmetry is
  CSG F\bar{4}3m   (216)
  MSG F\bar{4}'3m' (216.76)

  The point symmetry of the Ir (Wyckoff 16e) atom is
     CPG 3m         (one free param)
     MPG 3m'

The A_2u = GM2- modes are the ones leading to this symmetry
reduction.  SMODES determines that there are 3 A_2u modes:
  1 Ir
  1 Y
  1 O
